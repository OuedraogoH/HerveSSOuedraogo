Profile

An accomplished development practitioner and leader with over 20 years of experience, including 14 years within the United Nations system, spanning country, subregional, regional and headquarters levels, as well as the private sector, international financial institutions, academia and governments. He leads the preparation and implementation of strategic instruments (CCA, UNSDCF, CPF, UN INFO) and programmes for the Africa region of the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), advising on and overseeing an annual programme budget of USD 50 million and leading a team of more than 60 personnel across multiple countries. His expertise spans strategic planning, resource mobilization and systems-based practice to enable agrifood systems transformation.
